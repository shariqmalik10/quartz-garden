This site is built with [Quartz 5](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz), a static-site generator for digital gardens and connected notes. Content is plain Markdown. Search, backlinks, the explorer, and the graph are generated at build time.

The structure takes inspiration from [jzhao.xyz](https://jzhao.xyz): a personal site as an open invitation into work and ideas rather than a fixed resume page.

The source projects referenced throughout the site live on [GitHub](https://github.com/shariqmalik10).
