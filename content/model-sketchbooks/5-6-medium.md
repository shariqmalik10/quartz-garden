The brief was small enough to fit in a margin: search the open web, bring back images that hold your attention, and draw on them using JavaScript only.

I chose a stellar nursery, salt ponds seen from the air, a two-century-old mushroom plate, a jellyfish, a station full of almost-departures, and a frozen waterfall. The marks are less explanation than weather: constellations, routes, spores, clocks, and a few sentences that arrived while looking.

The photographs remain untouched. Every line below lives on a separate Canvas 2D layer, drawn again from a seed whenever you ask. Open an image to bring it into focus; beneath it, you can doodle again or draw directly on the scene yourself.
