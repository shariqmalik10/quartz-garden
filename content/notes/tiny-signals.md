Some of the most useful signals in a system arrive quietly: a reconciliation total that drifts, a repeated support question, or a small pause before someone trusts a dashboard.

This is a **sample field note**. It is here to show the shape of the notebook while the garden is still young—short enough to scan, but connected enough to grow later.

## What I am noticing

- A healthy system explains itself before someone has to ask.
- Small mismatches are often more useful than large averages.
- Friction is evidence. It points toward a missing definition, affordance, or feedback loop.

> The quiet parts of a product are often where trust is actually being negotiated.
