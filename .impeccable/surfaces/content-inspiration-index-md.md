Scope: `/inspiration`; visitor mode: Read.

Visitors need to find a useful saved resource quickly among hundreds of links, while still being able to wander by category.

Direction: a garden atlas ledger. A prominent search field, live result count, and compact category index sit above native expandable groups. Filtering opens matching groups and hides irrelevant links; without JavaScript, every group remains browsable through native details controls.

Memorable moment: typing a few letters turns the full atlas into a small, legible trail without navigating away.

Constraints: keep all links as ordinary external anchors, preserve the generated Signal Atlas workflow, avoid a card dashboard, support keyboard navigation and screen-reader status updates, and collapse cleanly to one column on mobile.
